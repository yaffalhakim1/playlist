# Brief — "Playlist"

## What it is
A native desktop player for YouTube playlists. Paste a link; the whole
playlist plays, in order, as audio only. No webview, no browser.

## Audience
Someone at a desk who wants music running while they work, and does not want
to manage a queue. They open it, and it is where they left it.

## Adjectives
Quiet · warm · continuous · native

## The one thing to say
Not "music" — every competitor says music. **Continuity**: it plays through,
it remembers, it resumes. One unbroken thing.

## Clichés to avoid (the category is saturated)
Music note · vinyl disc · play triangle in a circle · headphones · equaliser
bars · sound waves · cassette reels · a literal list/menu icon.

## Where it must work
32 px in the notification area, 16 px as a favicon, one colour, on a warm
terracotta or on white. Also as an app icon at 512 px.

## Assumptions (stated, not asked)
- The name stays "Playlist"; the mark is a symbol first, with a wordmark later.
- It should sit with Onevra's warm palette and Inter, not fight them.
- A tray icon exists today as a hand-drawn music note; it is a placeholder and
  may be replaced.

## Word map
name/offering → a run, a ribbon, a thread, a seam, a stitch, a queue, a groove
adjectives → unbroken, even, calm, folded, continuous
promise → "it keeps going; you don't touch it"
opposites → shuffle, random, single track, video, cloud

Intersections worth drawing:
1. **play + unbroken** → a play mark made of one continuous line
2. **a list + one stroke** → a queue drawn as a single folded band
3. **the letter** → a P whose bowl is a play mark
