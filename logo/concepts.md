and small-size strength (1–5 each, out of 25).

## The three that go forward

**1 — One Stroke Play** (22). Strongest: it says *play* instantly and
*unbroken* on second look, it is one continuous path so it survives 16 px, and
nothing in the category uses it. Risk: a curled play mark can read as a
"refresh" arrow if the tail is too long.

**2 — The Ribbon Queue** (21). The most distinctive, and the most literal to
the product: a queue is a sequence, and a folded band is a sequence. Risk: at
16 px three folds is one fold too many; needs a small-size cut.

**3 — P / Play** (20). The safest and the most "brand": it is the name. Risk:
the P-and-play combination is close to several video apps, and a P with a
triangle in its bowl is the most obvious idea in the world — it needs a
non-obvious cut to be ownable.
